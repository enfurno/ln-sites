The Legend of Canrock

A real bull who has passed on to lush pastures beyond the arena.

The namesake

Gentle strength

Canrock's Grindhouse & Tavern is elevated American rodeo casual dining, built around the soul of a real bull named Canrock. Recognizable by his striking russet-tan body, deep chocolate-brown head and shoulders, pale pink speckled muzzle, and wide, sweeping horns, Canrock commanded respect whether standing quietly behind a pasture fence or inspiring stories around the ranch gate.

Rather than a generic western theme, every detail honors his actual likeness: his proud horn span defines our emblem, and his speckled muzzle and broad forehead inspire our neon sign.

Est. mid-1880s heritage

Rooted in Oklahoma rodeo

Rodeo means "roundup." Before statehood, ranch hands turned everyday roping and bronc-riding into competitions rooted in Mexican vaquero traditions.

Legends

Oklahoma forged champions like seven-time world champion Jim Shoulders, Freckles Brown, and Lane Frost. Our Hall of Champions honors them alongside Canrock.
