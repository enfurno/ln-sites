In Loving Memory

A real bull.
A real legend.

Canrock's is built around the soul of a real bull named Canrock: russet-tan body, deep chocolate-brown head and shoulders, pale pink speckled muzzle, and wide, sweeping horns. Every detail honors his likeness, resilience and gentle strength, turning the restaurant into a warm gathering hall where ranch hands, families and rodeo fans raise a glass to his memory.
